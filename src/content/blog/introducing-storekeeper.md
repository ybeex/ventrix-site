Every store has the same Monday morning. Someone needs the angle grinder, nobody is sure who had it last, and the sign-out sheet has a name on it that might be "Tunde" or might be "Tobi". Twenty minutes later the grinder turns up on a bench in the next building.

Ventrix exists to end that conversation.

## What Ventrix is

Ventrix is a web app for tracking tools, equipment and stock. It runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer, so there is nothing to install and no scanner to buy. It does a small number of things, and tries to do them properly:

- **Records who has what.** Check items out to a person, a team, a department or a job site, with a due date, and check them back in with a note on their condition.
- **Warns you before stock runs out.** Consumables have a minimum and a reorder quantity. You see "near minimum" and "low" while there is still time to buy.
- **Keeps counts honest.** Count one shelf at a time, explain every difference, and let a supervisor approve adjustments.
- **Works offline.** A local copy of your items and people means search works without a connection. What you do while offline waits in a queue and syncs safely when you return.
- **Gives you control.** Four roles, an approval policy you set, optional photo proof, and quick PIN switching so a crew can share one tablet.

## Who it is for

If something leaves a shelf and should come back, or gets used up and should be reordered, Ventrix fits. That includes [tool rooms and stores](/use-cases/tool-rooms), [workshops](/use-cases/workshops), [schools and labs](/use-cases/schools) and [contractors and field crews](/use-cases/contractors).

It is not a warehouse management system. If you run a distribution centre with thousands of pallet movements a day, you need different software. Ventrix is for the counter, the bench and the van.

## The principles behind it

**Simple at the counter.** The person using it is busy and often holding a tool. Every screen is built for that moment first, with large buttons, search that finds things from a few letters, and as few steps as possible between "I have it" and "it's recorded".

**Honest records.** A record is only useful if it says what really happened. Every action carries a name. Differences found in a count need a reason. The activity log can't be edited by the people it describes.

**Works where you work.** Stockrooms, yards and basements lose signal. If software only works with a perfect connection, people go back to paper. Ventrix is built so that losing the connection doesn't stop the work.

**Control you can dial.** A five-person workshop doesn't need approval chains. A larger store might want a supervisor to approve every stock adjustment and a photo for every check-out. Both are fine, and you choose in settings.
